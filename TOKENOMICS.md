### [EN] Token Concept
$SEED is a utility token of the Green Titans protocol. The token is backed exclusively by State PPP (Public-Private Partnership) contracts, ensuring its stability and legitimacy as a digital asset.

**Key Metrics:**
* **Token Name:** SEED Coin
* **Symbol:** $SEED
* **Blockchain:** Solana
* **Guarantee:** Backed exclusively by State PPP contracts.
