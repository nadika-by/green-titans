## 🇺🇸 English Version

In the Green Titans ecosystem, the State is not just a regulator — it is the **Foundational Guardian**. Like a divine force, it provides the "Hands" within which the circular economy can exist safely and efficiently.

### 1. The Suppliers (The Flow of Life)
* **Sources:** Households, agriculture (animals/birds), industrial enterprises, and institutions.
* **Input:** The inevitable waste generated by the vital activity of a developing society.

### 2. The Transformer: Green Titans (Under State Protection)
* **Role:** We act as the vital bridge. Supported by **State-Private Partnership (PPP)**, we intercept the waste flow.
* **Trust:** The State provides the legal "Cradle," while Solana Blockchain provides the technical transparency.

### 3. The Customer: Sustainable Business
* **Recipients:** Manufacturing enterprises requiring high-quality secondary raw materials.
* **Output:** We deliver processed, ready-to-use resources, turning a liability into a profitable asset.
